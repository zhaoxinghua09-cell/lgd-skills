Why publish this instead of sending them issues

Because an issue we file is a request; a public report with a reproducible runner is evidence. Anyone — including the maintainers — can rerun the command and check whether we are right. If we are wrong about a single item, the runner will show it.

These are third-party projects. We are not claiming they are bad packages. A score of 20 means a stranger cannot verify the claims in ten minutes, which is a property of the package's evidence, not of its usefulness.

Measures verifiability, not usefulness or popularity. Low score means evidence is missing, not that the skill is bad. Snapshot at a point in time; the sample is not random. Data: leaderboard.json · Machine entry: llms.txt · Request a review: open an issue
